# PortfolioIQ

**A snapshot-based tool for understanding your own investments, built around a real CSV importer.**

Most people who hold investments don't have an easy way to actually understand what they hold: positions scattered across statements and brokerage exports, inconsistent cost basis, no single place to see the full picture. PortfolioIQ is built around a CSV importer as the core of the product: you import brokerage export files, the importer maps inconsistent columns, and you choose whether each row should Add, Update, or Skip an existing position — with bulk editing for handling many rows at once. Where cost-basis or gain data is genuinely missing, it shows an explicit "gain not available" state instead of inventing a number.

**Status:** prototype — a snapshot-based educational and analysis tool, not a live brokerage-connected platform.
