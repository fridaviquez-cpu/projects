# EnviroTrap Nexus

**UIUC Waste Intelligence Platform — from collection to characterization to action.**

A sustainability technology initiative focused on institutional waste measurement, analytics, contamination research, and data-driven waste-management improvements. Built for the IBM AI for Good Challenge: a three-tier attribution engine (RFID lookup, GPS nearest-centroid model, and an AI fallback) closes the gap on UIUC LoadMan pickup records that lost their building attribution after the truck RFID readers degraded to 0% detection in 2022.
