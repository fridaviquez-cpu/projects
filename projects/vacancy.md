# Vacancy

**The seat finds you, the wall keeps you — a desk-finding and focus companion for a campus engineering library.**

Vacancy is a concept for finding and keeping a focus seat in a crowded engineering library: a pocket app for finding and claiming a desk, plus a companion screen built into the desk itself. The pocket app lets a student browse the library by floor and filter by outlet access or quiet zone, with open seats shown on a color-coded map (green = available, amber = held, red = occupied). At the desk, a companion screen (with a small robot mascot, Vee) confirms the seat and runs a focus timer via a physical dial.

**Status:** interactive prototype built on simulated library data — not yet connected to live occupancy. Built as part of TE 230.
