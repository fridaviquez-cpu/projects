# BECE Bridge

**Course-grounded study support for UIUC ECE students, built around real lecture material.**

UIUC's electrical and computer engineering courses move fast, and most study tools are generic — not grounded in the actual lectures, problem sets, and scope a student is working from. BECE Bridge is built around pilot course workspaces spanning ECE 110, 120, 210, 220, 310, 313, 329, 330, 333, 340, and 342. A student uploads their own lecture material into a course's workspace, then asks follow-up questions grounded in that content; the system responds with interactive visuals alongside the explanation, and can generate study plans, flashcards, cheat sheets, and practice exams from the same material.

**Status:** strong prototype — the core loop (upload, ask grounded questions, generate study tools) works across 11 pilot course workspaces; passed 35 API/parser launch-readiness tests.
