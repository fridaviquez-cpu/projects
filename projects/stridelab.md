# StrideLab

**Track your runs. Understand your progress. Train with purpose.**

StrideLab is a running companion that brings your training log, progress charts, workout planner, and AI-powered training journal into one place. Log runs manually or import Strava screenshots, set personal goals, and get coaching suggestions based on your recent training and progress. Customize your running buddy for extra motivation, and optionally create an account to keep your training log synced across devices.

StrideLab complements apps like Strava — it doesn't record live GPS runs.

- 🔗 **Live demo:** [stride-lab.replit.app](https://stride-lab.replit.app/)
