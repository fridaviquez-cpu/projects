# Tau Tactics

**A focus and study companion, Leo the lion included, built as a take-home resource for students.**

Most productivity apps reduce focus to a timer, or lean on gamification that ends up rewarding screen time instead of actual learning. Tau Tactics is a study and focus resource built around Plan, Focus, Reflect, Practice, Improve: customizable focus and break timers with saved presets, alongside Leo, a lion study companion who grows through completed sessions and meaningful learning check-ins rather than raw time on screen. Longer sessions aren't rewarded more than shorter, well-used ones, and missed days don't weaken Leo.

**Status:** limited beta — working custom timer, saved browser-local progress, Leo's progression system, learning reflections, and a mobile-ready Focus experience.
