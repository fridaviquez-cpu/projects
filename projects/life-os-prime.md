# Life OS Prime

**A personal performance dashboard for academics, ventures, leadership, health, and faith.**

Tracks eight interconnected systems through a short onboarding wizard, a daily check-in, habit streaks, a founder tracker pre-loaded with real ventures, a priority triage tool with its classification rule shown in plain text, and weekly and monthly reviews. A few screens can ask Claude for a grounded strategic insight built from exactly what's logged, not a scripted-sounding paragraph. Everything is stored only in the browser.

This is the new and improved version of an earlier, now-archived project — [`life-os`](https://github.com/tjppppppppp/life-os) — rebuilt from scratch as a Claude Artifact rather than a gamified static app.

**Status:** prototype — evidence-based by design, every score comes from logged data or says `INSUFFICIENT DATA` instead of guessing.
